01Finance

Accounting and tax, handled as one job.

Outsourced accounting, reporting and tax for businesses that would rather not build a finance department yet. One team keeps your books, reports on them and files from them.

Talk to us about your accounts

  • Bookkeeping

    Transactions recorded and reconciled every period, so the ledger agrees with the bank.

  • Management accounts

    A regular pack covering profit and loss, balance sheet and cash, with notes on what changed and why.

  • Year-end accounts

    Annual accounts prepared from books we already know, with the workings attached.

  • Tax returns

    Returns prepared and filed for the business, with the calculation set out so you can follow it.

02Technology R&D

Find out whether it works before you fund it.

Contract research for teams with an idea that needs evidence. Bring us a technical question and we answer it with a feasibility study, a prototype, or both.

Talk to us about a research question

  1. Step 1

    The question

    We agree the one question the work has to answer, and what would count as a yes.

  2. Step 2

    Feasibility study

    Desk research, analysis and small experiments that test whether the idea can work.

  3. Step 3

    Prototype

    A working model that proves the riskiest part first.

  4. Step 4

    Report

    Method, results and a plain recommendation to build, change or stop.
